## Account Memory

Skills for keeping a persistent per-account record — logging interactions, tracking commitments, and portfolio triage

- [**log-interaction**](../skills/account-memory/log-interaction/SKILL.md) — Updates an Account Manager's persistent account record from a call transcript, call notes, email thread or chat export — pasted in, or pulled from a connected meeting notetaker such as Granola when the AM has allowed it in their settings. It pulls out new commitments (ours and theirs), stakeholder changes, issues and a timeline entry, shows the changes for the AM to confirm, then produces the updated record.
- [**portfolio-triage**](../skills/account-memory/portfolio-triage/SKILL.md) — Answers "which of my accounts need me this week, and why" for an Account Manager, by checking every account record they have against a fixed set of rules — overdue commitments, accounts gone quiet, renewals coming up, issues left open too long, and champions who have left — and ranking the accounts with the specific reasons and one next step each.
